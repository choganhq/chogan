Eleven new games: backgammon, mancala, nonogram, FreeCell, Reversi, Nine Men's Morris, peg solitaire, battleship, bridges, Code Breaker and breakout. Each one has a daily challenge and its own awards; the board games play two players on one phone or against the computer at three levels.
In two-player dots and boxes, the first player is now called Player 1 instead of You.
