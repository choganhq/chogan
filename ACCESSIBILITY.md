# Accessibility

Chogan should be playable by as many people as possible. This page says honestly what works today, what does not, and how to tell us when something stands in your way.

## What works

- **Languages and direction.** The interface is in Persian (right to left), English, Simplified Chinese, German, Spanish and Polish, and follows the device language on first start.
- **Light and dark themes**, or follow the system. Text on a game's colour picks white or dark ink by contrast in the dark theme.
- **Less motion.** When the system asks for reduced motion, the app's animations and confetti are turned off.
- **Sound and vibration** each have their own switch in the settings, and no game needs sound to be played.
- **No time pressure in most games.** Puzzles, card and board games wait for you; timed modes, where they exist, are optional and off by default. The action games (Block Drop, Breakout, Ping Pong, Air Hockey and similar) do need quick reactions.
- **Keyboard.** Touch comes first, but most games can also be played with a keyboard; each game's «?» help lists its keys.
- **Offline.** Everything works without a connection, so nothing fails on a slow or metered network.

## Known limitations

- **Screen readers.** The menus, buttons and dialogs have labels, but most game boards are drawn on a canvas, which a screen reader cannot read. The games are not playable without sight today.
- **Colour.** A few games use colour to tell pieces apart (for example Ludo and Chain Reaction). Most pair colour with shape or position, but not all of them.
- **Zoom.** Pinch-zoom is turned off so that a swipe on a board is not taken as a zoom. Games scale to fill the screen instead, and Mahjong has its own zoom.
- **Fine movement.** Some games ask for drags or quick taps (Pool, Darts, Gorillas). Where a second control exists, such as buttons for angle and power, it is behind the game's gear.

## Tell us

If something keeps you from playing, please [open an issue](https://github.com/choganhq/chogan/issues/new/choose) or write to choganhq@gmail.com, in any language. Say which game, which device or browser, and what assistive technology you use, if any. Reports like this are treated as bugs, not wishes.
